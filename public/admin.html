Categories

Rename a category to change its name. Rename it to the name of another one to merge them, for example "drinks" into "Drinks". A category can be deleted once no dish uses it.

QR code

Customers scan this to open the menu on their phone. No app to install. Print it and put it on the tables. You can also make a QR for a single dish: it opens that dish's detail card.

3D view

Spin it around, or see it on your table.

Drag to rotate. Tap "View on your table" to see it at real size through your camera. The dish appears once your phone has found your table; until then you will see a scanner. If that button is missing, this phone or browser does not support AR.

Tips for placing it on your table
  1. Tap View on your table (AR) and allow camera access.
  2. Hold the phone about an arm's length from the table and move it slowly side to side for a few seconds.
  3. Keep the camera on the table top, not the floor. While the scanner shows, the dish stays hidden on purpose.
  4. When the dish appears, drag it to move it and turn it with two fingers.

Works best in good light. Plain, shiny or dark tables are hard to detect, so try placing a napkin, menu or coaster with a pattern on the table and aim at it.
